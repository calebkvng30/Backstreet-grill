The Backstreet

A backstreet warehouse, turned steakhouse.

Sliding barn doors open onto a room built around fire — jazz on the walls, a wine wall stacked floor to ceiling, and a black iron grill running the length of the kitchen.

Every steak is finished on the iron and rested tableside. Pair it off the wine wall or start with a mezze platter built for the table to share.
